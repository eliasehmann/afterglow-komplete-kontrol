Tips
  • Colours flicker or get replaced? Close the Komplete Kontrol app and your music software's keyboard integration: they control the lights too.
  • Using the Kontrol LEDs app? It keeps running in the system tray, next to the clock. Right-click its icon to start it with Windows or to quit.
  • Your last look comes back automatically the next time you start.
